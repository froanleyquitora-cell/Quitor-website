About Bukidnon Milk Company

Bukidnon Milk Company (BMC) raises about 2,000 cows on a 460-hectare grazing farm in Maramag, Bukidnon. Our premium milk is sold through select Balai ni Fruitas, Landers, S&R, and SM outlets and supplies cafés, restaurants, bakeries, and retailers across the country.

With sales and distribution points in Maramag, Cagayan de Oro, and Davao City, we are growing our reach and getting ready for new dairy products like cheese and yogurt.
